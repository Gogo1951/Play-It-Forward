# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown when you open a mailbox, finds a player who can use each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try adding guilds by name, the reasons now shown in each recipient list, and Rescan for Better Match on WoW Forever.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On **My Guilds**, type a guild you're not in into **Add Guild**, press Enter, then press **Find Recipients** at a mailbox: its online members should get gear ahead of strangers.
2. On **My Guilds**, untick **Include This Guild** or click the remove icon beside a guild, then press **Find Recipients** again without reloading: its members should drop off.
3. Hover the **Play It Forward** button beside Blizzard's mail frame and try both **Open Distribution Window** and **Options** in its menu.
4. Click a recipient and check the top of the list says how many candidates there are and which classes the item suits best.
5. On WoW Forever, click a recipient and press **Rescan for Better Match**: a search should start, with no error.
6. Tick **Disenchant** at the top of the mail window, pick one of your characters, and check unmatched gear moves to its own **Disenchant** section.
7. Mail something to one of your own alts: it should arrive titled with the item name and no note, and the letter should disappear once the item is taken.

## Everything Else

8. Walk up to a mailbox with an outgrown bind-on-equip green in your bags: the window should open by itself.
9. Press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), untick anything you'd rather keep, then press **Distribute** and click Accept on each popup.
10. Log in once on an alt, then bring gear that suits it to a mailbox on another character: it should go to that alt ahead of strangers.
11. Click a recipient to pick someone else or **Keep Item**, press **Find Recipients** again, and check your choice stays.
12. Type **/pif**, and also try Options > AddOns > Play It Forward: the options should open inside Blizzard's Options window.
13. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
14. In a city, hover yourself and another Play It Forward player to see your **Badge of Generosity** totals; on WoW Forever, also hover someone **in combat** and check no error appears.
