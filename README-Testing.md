# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown when you open a mailbox, finds a player of the right class for each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try it on WoW Forever, on a non-English client, and with the new Food, Drink, Potions, and Scrolls switches.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. On WoW Forever, where Play It Forward is new, give away one item at a mailbox from start to finish.
2. On a non-English client, read the mail window, the options, and the chat messages: everything should be in your language except the letter strangers receive.
3. Take a stat scroll you've outgrown, such as a Scroll of Agility, to a mailbox and check it goes to a class that uses that stat.
4. Untick Gear, Consumables, Food, Drink, Potions, or Scrolls at the top of the mail window: those items leave the list, and the same switch in **/pif** unticks too.
5. Every row now starts ticked, so untick one, press **Find Recipients** again, and check it stays unticked.

## Everything Else

6. Walk up to a mailbox with an outgrown bind-on-equip green in your bags, and the window should open by itself.
7. Press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), then press **Distribute** and accept each popup.
8. Check each recipient suits their item, such as cloth with Intellect going to a mage, priest, or warlock, and click a name to pick someone else or **Keep Item**.
9. Type **/pif**, and also try Options > AddOns > Play It Forward: the options should open inside Blizzard's Options window, not floating on their own, especially on TBC Anniversary.
10. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
11. After a gift, stand in a city and hover yourself to see your Generosity totals, then hover another Play It Forward player to see theirs.
12. On WoW Forever, duel someone in a city and hover them mid-fight: no error should appear.
