# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown when you open a mailbox, finds a player of the right class for each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try giving away weapon oils and sharpening stones.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Carry a few low-level sharpening stones, weightstones, wizard oils, or mana oils to a mailbox and check they're listed and go to fitting classes: stones to melee fighters, wizard oils to casters, mana oils to anyone with mana.
2. Untick **Oils & Stones** at the top of the mail window, and check they drop off the list and the same switch in Options shows unticked.
3. On TBC Anniversary, bring a spell damage or healing green, such as one "of Healing", and check it goes to a caster or healer rather than under **Stats Couldn't Be Read**.
4. On a Korean client, check gear with ordinary stats finds recipients rather than landing under **Stats Couldn't Be Read**.

## Everything Else

5. Walk up to a mailbox with an outgrown bind-on-equip green in your bags, and the window should open by itself.
6. Press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), then press **Distribute** and accept each popup.
7. Check each recipient suits their item, such as cloth with Intellect going to a mage, priest, or warlock, and click a name to pick someone else or **Keep Item**.
8. Click a recipient, choose **Find Recipients for This Item**, and keep pressing **Find Recipients** until a better-suited player turns up or the search stops.
9. Untick a row or one of the switches at the top of the mail window, press **Find Recipients** again, and check it stays unticked.
10. Type **/pif**, and also try Options > AddOns > Play It Forward: the options should open inside Blizzard's Options window, especially on TBC Anniversary.
11. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
12. After a gift, stand in a city and hover yourself to see your Generosity totals, then hover another Play It Forward player to see theirs.
13. On WoW Forever, duel someone in a city and hover them mid-fight: no error should appear.
