# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown when you open a mailbox, finds a player who can use each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try mailing your own alts and guildmates, and playing on Mists of Pandaria Classic.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Log in once on each of a few alts, then bring gear one of them suits to a mailbox: it should go to that alt ahead of strangers.
2. On **My Alts**, pick a character, change its specs, **Gear Levels** or **Include This Character**, and check the mailbox follows suit.
3. On **My Guilds**, untick **Include** beside a guildmate (a guild bank works well) and check they're never offered anything at the mailbox.
4. Tick **Disenchant** at the top of the mail window, pick one of your characters, and check gear nobody wanted moves to **Matched** under that character's name.
5. Write your own subject and note on **Mail Message Text**, then check a gift to one of your alts arrives just as the preview showed.
6. Close the mail window at a mailbox, then click the **Play It Forward** button beside Blizzard's mail frame to bring it back.
7. On Mists of Pandaria Classic, run a full mailbox trip and check gear goes to classes and specs that can use it.

## Everything Else

8. Walk up to a mailbox with an outgrown bind-on-equip green in your bags: the window should open by itself.
9. Follow the line above the buttons: press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), then **Distribute**, and click Accept on each popup.
10. Check each recipient suits their item, and click a name to pick someone else, **Keep Item**, or **Find Recipients for This Item**.
11. Untick a row or one of the switches at the top of the mail window, press **Find Recipients** again, and check it stays unticked.
12. Type **/pif**, and also try Options > AddOns > Play It Forward: the options should open inside Blizzard's Options window.
13. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
14. In a city, hover yourself and another Play It Forward player to see your **Badge of Generosity** totals; on WoW Forever, also hover someone mid-duel and check no error appears.
