# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown at a mailbox, finds a player who can use each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try the new gift tab on the mailbox, stopping a run partway, and the new Keep in Bags and Never Give Away choices.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, WoW Forever, and Mists of Pandaria Classic.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. At a mailbox, click the gift tab on the mail frame's right edge to open the window, and click it again to close it.
2. Check the gift tab sits right on ElvUI and on TSM's mail window, and follows when you switch with TSM's **WoW UI** and **TSM** buttons.
3. Drag the window by its title bar, then hover the gift tab and pick **Reset Window Position**: it should go back beside the mailbox.
4. Press **Distribute** with several items ticked, then press **Stop**: sending should halt and chat should say how many went.
5. Check each item you send moves to a **Sent** section at the bottom of the list, showing who got it.
6. On **What to Give Away**, set **Keep in Bags** beside **Food**, then check only the stacks above that amount are listed.
7. Click a recipient and pick **Never Give Away**: the item should leave the list and appear on **What to Give Away**, where its remove icon offers it again.
8. Ask a friend to return a gift you mailed them, then open a mailbox: chat should tell you to take it back from your inbox.

## Everything Else

9. Press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), untick anything you'd rather keep (a section heading's tick does its whole section), then press **Distribute** and click Accept on each popup.
10. Log in once on an alt, then bring gear that suits it to a mailbox on another character: it should go to that alt ahead of strangers.
11. Click a recipient to pick someone else or **Keep Item**, press **Find Recipients** again, and check your choice stays.
12. Open the options with **/pif** and with **Play It Forward Options** in the gift tab's menu: both should open inside Blizzard's Options window.
13. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
14. In a city, hover yourself and another Play It Forward player to see your **Badge of Generosity** totals; on WoW Forever, also hover someone **in combat** and check no error appears.
