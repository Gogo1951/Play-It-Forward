# Play It Forward // Test Plan

Play It Forward lists the gear and consumables you've outgrown when you open a mailbox, finds a player of the right class for each one, and mails it to them. This time we most want you to try the smarter **Find Recipients for This Item** search and the new Feedback & Support page.

**Play on Classic Era, Season of Discovery, TBC Anniversary, and WoW Forever.** If something looks wrong, tell us the check number and what you saw on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eh8hKq992Q).

## What's New

1. Click a recipient, choose **Find Recipients for This Item**, then keep pressing **Find Recipients**: the best-suited class should be tried first (warriors before paladins for plate with Strength), and the search should stop once nobody better could turn up.
2. Open Options > AddOns > Play It Forward > Feedback & Support, check the Discord, CurseForge, Wago and GitHub links and the version line, and copy one link with Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C.

## Everything Else

4. Walk up to a mailbox with an outgrown bind-on-equip green in your bags, and the window should open by itself.
5. Press **Find Recipients** (again if nobody turns up), then press **Distribute** and accept each popup.
6. Check each recipient suits their item, such as cloth with Intellect going to a mage, priest, or warlock, and click a name to pick someone else or **Keep Item**.
7. Untick a row or one of the switches at the top of the mail window, press **Find Recipients** again, and check it stays unticked.
8. Type **/pif**, and also try Options > AddOns > Play It Forward: the options should open inside Blizzard's Options window, not floating on their own, especially on TBC Anniversary.
9. Type **/pif** in combat: the options should stay shut and chat should say why.
10. After a gift, stand in a city and hover yourself to see your Generosity totals, then hover another Play It Forward player to see theirs.
11. On WoW Forever, duel someone in a city and hover them mid-fight: no error should appear.
