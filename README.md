# Play It Forward

Mail outgrown BoE gear, food, potions, scrolls, oils, and stones to your alts, guildmates, and leveling players. Every item goes to the class it suits best, turning bag clutter into somebody else's next upgrade. Pay it forward, one green at a time.

**TL;DR:** Those greens you'll never wear could make a lowbie's whole week. Open a mailbox, press two buttons, and every spare lands with someone who can actually use it.

## Setup

1. Install the add-on, ideally using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/play-it-forward) or [Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/play-it-forward).
2. Type `/pif` to open the Options Interface. The defaults are ready to go, so a peek is all it needs.
3. Visit a mailbox. If you're carrying something worth passing on, the window opens by itself.
4. Press **Find Recipients**, untick anything you'd rather keep, then press **Distribute**.
5. _"It's dangerous to go alone! Take this."_

## Features

📬 **Ready at the Mailbox** // Open any mailbox and everything you've outgrown is already listed, so two clicks send it all on its way.

🎯 **Right Gear, Right Player** // Your alts and guildmates first, then strangers questing in zones made for their level. Every piece goes to a class that will actually wear it.

♻️ **Nothing Goes to Waste** // Gear nobody can use can head to your enchanter alt for disenchanting instead of rotting in your bags.

💛 **Badge of Generosity** // Keeps a lifetime tally of everything you've given away, and shows off the totals of other givers around town.

🦺 **Safety First** // Only tradeable bind-on-equip gear up to the rarity you pick, plus spare consumables. Never soulbound or quest items, and anything you untick stays put.

## Tips & Tricks

### At the Mailbox

* Click any recipient to pick someone else, search for just that item, or keep it.
* Closed the window? The button beside Blizzard's mail frame brings it back.
* Using TSM's mailbox? Switch to Blizzard's Send Mail panel to distribute, since TSM's doesn't take attachments.

### What Lands in Their Mailbox

A parcel titled **Play It Forward!** with a friendly note, so a gift from a stranger never reads like a scam:

> Just a little something to help you level. (=
>
> No strings attached. Use it if you can, or sell it. Don't want it? Hit Return and it'll find a new home.

Rather say it your way? Write your own on the **Mail Message Text** panel.
